# User Guide — SA Property Auctions

**Audience:** Public beta users  
**Version:** RC6

## Getting started

1. Open the site and browse auctions on the home page.  
2. Create an account at **Register** (accept Terms & Privacy).  
3. Verify your email if prompted.  
4. Sign in and optionally save favourites / saved searches.  
5. Upgrade on **Pricing** for Premium AI Search and higher limits.

## Searching

- Use filters (province, type, price) for reliable results.  
- Premium AI Search understands natural language (e.g. “4 bedroom house in Pretoria”).  
- Listing data can change — verify with the auctioneer/sheriff before bidding.

## Alerts & account

- Manage profile name and subscription from **Profile**.  
- **Export my data** downloads a JSON copy of your personal platform data.  
- **Delete account** permanently removes your login (type `DELETE` to confirm). Cancel billing first if subscribed.

## Billing

- Checkout is secured by Stripe.  
- Cancel or update payment methods via **Manage billing** on Profile when available.  
- Policies: Subscription & Billing, Refunds & Cancellation (footer).

## Privacy & support

- Privacy Policy, POPIA Notice, Cookie Policy — footer.  
- Privacy requests: `/privacy-requests` or `privacy@sapropertyauctions.co.za`.  
- Support: `/contact` or `info@sapropertyauctions.co.za`.
